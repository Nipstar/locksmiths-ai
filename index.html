Your Hands Are in a Lock When the Phone Goes

You're halfway through a lock change. Or under a bonnet on an auto job. Or on a job across town. The phone goes. By the time you ring back, they've already called the next locksmith on Google and been booked in. Most people don't leave a voicemail. They just move down the list.

The 11pm Lockout Rings Out

Someone's stood on their doorstep at 11pm. Or a car key has snapped in the ignition on a Sunday. Or there's been a break-in and the locks need doing tonight. These are your best jobs, and they come when you're asleep or out for the evening. Every ring that goes unanswered is a job for whoever picks up.

A lockout and a quote for a lock upgrade are not the same call. But out of hours, nobody's there to tell them apart.
